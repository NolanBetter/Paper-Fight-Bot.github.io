FAQ

If your question isn't here, ask in Discord.

Does it need any other plugins?

No, on anything newer than 3.17.1. It is one jar.

3.17.1 and older ran bots as disguised zombies, so those need LibsDisguises. That is the version to use if your server is below 1.20.5.

What can I run it on?

Paper 1.20.5 or newer, or a fork like Purpur. Nothing else.

Spigot, CraftBukkit and Folia are not supported. FightBot checks at startup and says so in the console rather than half working.

Why Paper and not Spigot?

Bots are real server side players now, not mobs wearing a disguise. They have real hitboxes, real physics, and every other plugin on your server sees them as players. They even show in the tab list with a realistic ping. That needs Paper's API.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free on Modrinth.

How many bots can I run at once?

Up to 200 from one command, with /fightbot spawn <count>. Whether your server enjoys 200 is another matter, since they path, fight and place blocks the way players do. Scale up gradually and watch your TPS.

Can bots fight each other?

Yes. Put them in groups and use /fightbot groupfight <group> <target>, or set two groups on one another. Good for watching what a loadout actually does.

Can I change their gear while they are fighting?

No. The bot is moving items between its hands the whole time, so its inventory is closed while it fights. Run /fightbot stop <bot> first, then right click it in creative or use Open gear in its settings menu.

Where did crystal PvP go?

It is switched off while it is reworked, and comes back in a later update. Roaming and patrolling are back as of 5.17.0 — see the wiki.

Does the mace work?

Yes. It is off by default, so turn it on with mace.enabled in the config, or for one bot in its settings menu.

A bot with a mace and wind charges wind-jumps and smashes you on the way down. With an elytra and rockets it takes off, flies over you and dives. If you dive on a bot, it sidesteps or gets its shield up.

Is there a permission node?

fightbot.use, default OP. Grant it to let other people spawn and command bots. /fightbot help in game lists everything.

Should I use the beta builds?

Only if you are chasing a specific fix or happy to report bugs. Stick to the newest full release for anything you care about, and back up first.

Can I use it in a video?

Go ahead. A link back is appreciated but not required.

How do I report a bug?

Use the report form. It takes your debug log and plugin list, and you will see any reply on your reports page. Discord works too.

Can I change settings without opening server files?

Yes, from 5.17.0. Type /fightbot web in game and open the link it gives you. It opens the editor on this site, where you can change any setting, watch the bots that are on, upload skins and voice lines, and read the debug logs.

The link is the key, so do not share it. It stops working after 30 minutes unused, after 4 hours, or when you type /fightbot web stop. Nothing on your server has to be opened up, and everything between your server and the page is encrypted with a key that is only in the link.

Do the bots talk?

With Simple Voice Chat on the server, yes. Bots show up in voice chat like players, and the ones that "talk" say lines by themselves when they join, start a fight, win one, get low or die.

You can add your own .mp3 or .wav files too. Those are never said by a bot on its own — only when someone types /fightbot vc <name> — everyone nearby is told it is a file added by that server and who played it, and every play is logged.
