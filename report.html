Report a bug

The more of this you fill in, the faster it gets fixed. Version numbers and console output do most of the work.

Run /fightbot debug on, make it happen again, then /fightbot debug off, and grab the file it names from plugins/FightBot/debug/.

What's in it: your FightBot, server and Java versions, the plugins your server runs, your worlds, your bots, config.yml (with keys and passwords hidden) and your server's TPS. Rather keep your plugins or TPS to yourself? Set debug.include.plugins: false or debug.include.tps: false in config.yml (FightBot 6.4.7 and up) before you start the log. Read it through before you send it: it goes with your report, so the FightBot team can see it.

Sent straight to the owner. You will see any reply on your reports page.

Before you send. If you are on a beta, try the newest full release first — a good share of reports are already fixed there. And check the FAQ, since slow bots and config changes not applying both have known answers.
