Install

Paper 1.20.5+, or a fork like Purpur. Four minutes to your first fight.

Which version do you need? Anything newer than 3.17.1 needs Paper 1.20.5 or newer, or a fork like Purpur, and nothing else installed. If your server is below 1.20.5, use 3.17.1 with the free LibsDisguises instead. Spigot, CraftBukkit and Folia are not supported either way.

Download the jar

Grab the newest full release from Modrinth. Betas are there too, but start on a stable build unless you are chasing a specific fix.

Open Modrinth

Drop it in and restart

Put the jar in your server's plugins folder and do a full restart, not a reload. The plugin writes its config.yml and botnames.yml on first boot.

On 3.17.1 or older, install the free LibsDisguises alongside it before restarting.

Spawn a bot

Join the server and run this. You get a bot with a random player‑style name at your feet.

/fightbot spawn

# or name it yourself
/fightbot spawn Kade

# or a whole crowd, up to 50
/fightbot spawn 10

Gear it up

Run /fightbot settings for a screen of every bot's head, click one, then Open gear. Or switch to creative and right‑click the bot. Give it armour, a weapon, and anything else you want it using. Or skip this and fight it bare‑handed.

Full slot list is in the wiki.

Point it at yourself

Now it hunts you.

/fightbot fight Kade YourUsername

# had enough
/fightbot stop
Permission: everything sits under fightbot.use, which defaults to OP. Grant it in your permissions plugin to let other people spawn bots.
