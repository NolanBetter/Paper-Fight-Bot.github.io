Import and export

Paste JSON from a backup, or plain Markdown where every ## heading starts a new entry and the text under it is the body.

How the two differ. A FAQ answer has headings switched off, because the question is already the heading. A wiki section has them on, so # gives you sub headings inside it, and the section title becomes its own button in the sidebar.
The list starts as what the site already shows. Every built in question and section is here to edit, so adding one adds to the list rather than replacing the page. Restore built in text puts the originals back whenever you want.
