Side by side

Paper Fight Bot next to the two plugins people usually weigh it against. Every line is what each project’s own page says — including the ones where they win.

Paper Fight Bot

This one. PvP bots that are real server‑side players, for Paper and Spigot servers. Free, no other plugins needed.

Its Modrinth page

FakePlayer

Carpet‑style utility bots for Paper: hold chunks loaded, stand in an AFK farm, be controlled a click at a time. Fighting is not what it is for.

Its Modrinth page

VexBot

The other PvP bot. A Paper plugin and a Fabric mod, so it runs in singleplayer too, with add‑ons for sims, waves and shops.

Its Modrinth page

So which one

You want something to fight

FightBot or VexBot. Both do real PvP. FightBot runs on Spigot and CraftBukkit as well, needs nothing else installed, and is changed from a web page rather than an in-game menu. VexBot also runs in singleplayer and on Fabric, which FightBot does not.

You want a body to hold a chunk

FakePlayer. That is what it is built for, and it does it with a small, sharp command set. FightBot’s bots can stand still, but a PvP plugin is a heavy way to keep a farm ticking.

You are filming or scripting

FightBot. ​/fightbot action moves a bot one key at a time with nothing automatic, five built‑in loadouts kit a bot in one click, and bots can walk on to the server by themselves while you record.
